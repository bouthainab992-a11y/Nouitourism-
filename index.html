8-Day Itinerary Overview

Day 1

Arrival in Moscow & Golden Ring Check-in

Flight with Turkish Airlines. Private transfer to 5-star Golden Ring Hotel located in central Moscow. Evening welcome briefing.

Day 2

Red Square, Kremlin & Moscow Cathedral Mosque

Guided tour of St. Basil Cathedral, Lenin Mausoleum, Kremlin grounds, followed by a visit to the architectural marvel of the Moscow Cathedral Mosque.

Day 3

Husky Dog Sledding & Winter Forest Safari

Excursion to the winter countryside. Experience traditional dog sledding through snowy woods, tea by campfire, and cultural photoshoots.

Day 4

Izmailovo Kremlin & Vernissage Artisan Market

Explore the fairytale wooden architecture of Izmailovo Kremlin. Free time to shop for authentic Russian crafts and souvenirs.

Day 5

Sparrow Hills (Colline des Moineaux) & Moscow Metro Tour

Enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of Moscow from Sparrow Hills near Moscow State University. Tour Moscow's legendary underground metro stations.

Days 6 & 7

Leisure, Shopping & Moskva River Cruise

Free time for high-end shopping at GUM mall and optional ice-breaker boat cruise along the Moskva River.

Day 8

Departure to Algeria

Breakfast at hotel, souvenir packing, and private airport transfer for returning Turkish Airlines flight.
